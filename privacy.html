Couch Remote for Mac

Privacy Policy

Effective August 2026

Couch Remote is a Mac utility that controls an Apple TV on your local network. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

What the app collects

Nothing. Couch Remote has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs. It never transmits any data to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your devices

  • Pairing credentials. When you pair with an Apple TV, the encryption keys that secure the connection are stored in your Mac's Keychain and used only to reconnect to that Apple TV.
  • Preferences. Settings such as the remote's position, opacity, and keyboard shortcuts are stored on your Mac.

Network activity

Couch Remote communicates only with the Apple TV you choose, directly over your local network using Bonjour discovery and Apple's Companion protocol, with the session encrypted end to end. It makes no other network connections.
