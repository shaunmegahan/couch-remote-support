SofaPilot Remote

Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

SofaPilot Remote is a desktop utility for controlling compatible streaming devices over your local network. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

What the app collects

Nothing. SofaPilot Remote has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs. It never transmits any data to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your devices

  • Pairing credentials. When you pair with a compatible device, the encryption keys that secure the connection are stored in your Keychain and used only to reconnect to that device.
  • Preferences. Settings such as the remote's position, opacity, and keyboard shortcuts are stored on your computer.

Network activity

SofaPilot Remote communicates only with the compatible Apple TV device you choose, directly over your local network using Bonjour discovery and the Companion protocol, with the session encrypted end to end. It makes no other network connections.
