Getting started

  1. Make sure your Mac and Apple TV are on the same local network.
  2. Open Couch Remote from the TV icon in your Mac's menu bar.
  3. Choose Devices, select your Apple TV, and enter the four-digit PIN shown on the television.

After the first pairing, Couch Remote stores the pairing keys in your Mac's Keychain and reconnects automatically.

Common questions

I don't see my Apple TV

Confirm that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi or Ethernet network. If macOS asks for Local Network access, allow it. Then reopen the device picker.

Where is the app?

Couch Remote is a menu-bar utility, so it does not appear in the Dock. Look for the TV icon in the menu bar and the small floating remote at the edge of your screen.

How do I change the floating remote?

Open Settings from the menu-bar remote or by right-clicking the floating remote. You can change its mode, position, opacity, keyboard shortcuts, and launch-at-login setting.

How do I pair again?

Open Devices and select the Apple TV again. Couch Remote will request a new PIN whenever a fresh pairing is required.
